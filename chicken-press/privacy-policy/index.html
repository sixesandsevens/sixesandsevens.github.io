Privacy Policy for Chicken Press

Effective date: May 7, 2026
Developer: Sixes & Sevens Studio
Contact: chris.tanton86@gmail.com

Chicken Press is a zine-making and print-layout app created by Sixes & Sevens Studio. This privacy policy explains how the app handles user data.

Summary

Chicken Press is designed to work locally on your device.

  • Chicken Press does not sell your data.
  • Chicken Press does not create user accounts.
  • Chicken Press does not upload your photos, PDFs, scans, or zine projects to Sixes & Sevens Studio servers.
  • Chicken Press does not use your images or documents for advertising or profiling.

Information Chicken Press Handles

Chicken Press may ask you to select or create content such as:

  • Photos or images
  • PDF files
  • Camera captures
  • Exported zine layouts or print-ready files

This content is used only so the app can create, format, preview, or export zine layouts.

Your selected files and images are processed on your device. They are not sent to Sixes & Sevens Studio unless you separately choose to send them outside the app using your device’s own sharing tools.

Camera and Photo Access

Chicken Press may request access to your camera or photos if you choose features such as taking pictures of pages, selecting images, importing files, or creating a zine from existing media.

The app only accesses the files or camera features that you choose to use. Camera and photo access is used for zine creation and formatting.

File Storage

Chicken Press may save exported files, project files, or temporary working files on your device, depending on the features you use.

You control these files through your device storage, file manager, gallery, or app settings. Deleting the app or clearing app data may remove files stored only inside the app.

Data Collection

Chicken Press does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, location, contacts, payment information, or messages.

At this time, Chicken Press does not use advertising trackers, analytics trackers, or user profiling tools.

Donations and External Links

Chicken Press may include links to external websites, such as Ko-fi or Sixes & Sevens Studio webpages.

When you open an external link, that website may collect information according to its own privacy policy. Sixes & Sevens Studio does not control the privacy practices of third-party websites.

If you leave a tip or donation through a third-party service, payment information is handled by that third-party service, not by Chicken Press.

Sharing

Chicken Press does not sell, rent, or share your personal data.

If you choose to export or share a zine, image, PDF, or other file using your device’s sharing options, that action is controlled by you and by the apps or services you choose to share with.

Children’s Privacy

Chicken Press is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because Chicken Press does not require accounts and does not intentionally collect personal information, Sixes & Sevens Studio does not knowingly collect personal data from children through the app.

Security

Chicken Press is designed to keep zine files and selected media local to your device. However, no app or device storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should avoid importing sensitive personal documents into any app unless you are comfortable storing and processing those files on your device.

Future Changes

Chicken Press may add new features in the future. If a future version changes how data is collected, used, stored, or shared, this privacy policy will be updated.

The latest version of this policy will be available at:

https://sixesandsevens.github.io/chicken-press/privacy-policy/
